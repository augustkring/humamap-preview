# BLENTERA Brand Strategy — Website

## Category
**Company AI foundation**

The category is deliberately concrete. BLENTERA is not positioned as an AI workforce, an agent-orchestration wrapper, an AI operating system slogan, a model reseller, a governance-only tower or a consultancy.

## Core proposition
**Build your company's AI capability once. Keep building on it.**

BLENTERA keeps company knowledge, work, governance and learning usable across people, agents, models and tools so new capability can start from what the company already knows, controls and has learned.

## Customer progress
Primary job:
- Increase useful AI capacity without losing company knowledge, control or continuity.

Priority gains:
- More useful capacity without linear coordination overhead.
- Less key-person and provider dependency.
- Faster onboarding for people, agents and new tools.
- Company capability that improves through reviewed use.
- Visible authority, approvals and evidence.

## Differentiation
BLENTERA is designed to own the persistent company layer, not the model.

The company layer persists across:
- employees,
- agents,
- models,
- runtimes,
- workflows,
- connected tools.

## Product pillars
1. **Knowledge foundation** — company foundation, connected knowledge, governed memory.
2. **Work & organisation** — goal-aware work, people + agent organisation, durable workflows.
3. **Compounding intelligence** — reviewed learning, reusable standards, AI-to-software.
4. **Control & portability** — permissions, approvals, evidence, runtime/provider freedom.

## Brand character
- Calm, not hyped.
- Exact, not magical.
- Product-led, not decorative.
- Premium SaaS, not “AI aesthetic”.
- European in operating discipline, not flag-waving.
- Cumulative: the visual and verbal system should reinforce persistence and reuse.

## Visual direction
- Neutral white / near-black base.
- Geist typography.
- Restrained borders and muted surfaces.
- No gradients, glows, orbs, particle fields or “neural network” imagery.
- No fake logos, testimonials or vanity metrics.
- Product UI must explain mechanics, not serve as decoration.
- One dominant idea per section.

## Verbal rules
Prefer:
- company AI foundation
- shared company context
- reviewed company memory
- real work
- reusable standards
- controlled autonomy
- provider portability
- stable work becomes software

Avoid:
- AI workforce
- agent army
- magic
- fully autonomous
- end-to-end AI
- guaranteed compliance
- revolutionary / 10x without evidence

## Public claim discipline
- Technical completion is not market proof.
- Illustrative interfaces must not be presented as customer evidence.
- Pricing is presented as launch planning while unvalidated.
- CTA remains “Join early access” while production qualification remains open.
- BLENTERA does not guarantee EU AI Act compliance.
