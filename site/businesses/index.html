Why now

Companies are changing faster. Human context matters more.

Roles change. Teams form and reform. People work across functions. AI changes how work is divided between people and technology.

The more dynamic the company becomes, the more valuable it is to understand the people doing the work and how they work together.

HumaMap gives that understanding somewhere to live.
Privacy, ownership & control

People stay in control of their information.

A HumaMap stays with the individual. Companies can use the information people choose to share, but the profile does not become company property.

Learn about privacy & security →
01

Your profile stays yours

02

You control what you share

Choose what is visible and who can see it, within the sharing settings used by your organisation.

03

Professional work context only

HumaMap is built around how people work professionally. It does not collect or infer GDPR Article 9 special-category data and is not a clinical or health assessment.

04

European infrastructure

Data is stored and processed in Europe, using European infrastructure across storage, databases, search and AI processing.

No advertising. No sale of personal data.
