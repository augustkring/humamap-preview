The understanding of people inside a company often lives in individual heads.

A manager learns who needs more context, who moves quickly, where friction may arise, who works well together and how different people need to be led.

Shared context stays with the company.

Useful to employees, managers and the next person who takes responsibility for the team.

People go on holiday. They get sick. They take parental leave. They change roles, retire or leave the company. When understanding lives only with them, part of it leaves too.

HumaMap makes that understanding shared, living and less dependent on any one person.

Why now

Companies are changing faster. Human context matters more.

Roles change. Teams form and reform. People work across functions. AI changes how work is divided between people and technology.

The organisation becomes more dynamic, but many companies still depend on informal knowledge about who works well with whom, who should own what and how different people need to be led.

The more work changes, the more valuable it becomes to understand the people doing it.HumaMap gives that understanding somewhere to live.
