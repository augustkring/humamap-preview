The missing layer

Add the human context behind the work.

Most companies already know people's roles, experience, skills, responsibilities and how the organisation is structured.

HumaMap adds what is harder to see: how people make decisions, what drives them, how they take ownership, communicate and work together.

It does not replace what your company already knows. It makes the decisions built on top of it better informed.

Turn what people know into something the company knows.

Managers build valuable understanding over time: who needs more context, who moves quickly, where friction may arise and how different people need to be led.

When that understanding only lives in one person's head, it is fragile. HumaMap makes it shared, living and less dependent on any one person.

Why now

Companies are changing faster. Human context matters more.

Roles change. Teams form and reform. People work across functions. AI changes how work is divided between people and technology.

The more dynamic the company becomes, the more valuable it is to understand the people doing the work and how they work together.

HumaMap gives that understanding somewhere to live.
