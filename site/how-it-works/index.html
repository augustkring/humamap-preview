Method

Built from established ways of understanding people at work.

HumaMap does not invent a new personality theory. It brings together professional information, recognised assessments and structured reflection, then organises the useful parts into a practical framework for work.

HumaMap CoreFoundation

The guided professional profile everyone starts with.

EvidenceOptional

Recognised evidence based assessments where they add useful information, for example Big Five instruments.

ReflectionOptional

Structured reflection frameworks can add another perspective, but HumaMap keeps them separate from evidence based assessment.

HumaMap organises the context into CAN, WILL and HOW.

CAN, WILL and HOW is HumaMap's organising framework. It is not presented as a new validated three factor scientific model.

CAN

What you bring.

Capabilities, knowledge, experience and the capacity to learn and solve problems.

O*NET Content Model ↗McCloy, Campbell & Cudeck, 1994 ↗
WILL

What moves you.

Motivation, initiative, priorities, direction and the willingness to take ownership and persist.

Performance determinants ↗Self Determination Theory at work ↗
HOW

How you work.

How you execute, communicate, collaborate, respond to feedback and influence the people around you.

O*NET Work Styles ↗Barrick & Mount, 1991 ↗
B = f(P, E)

People make more sense in context.

Kurt Lewin's person and environment tradition, and later person environment fit research, both reinforce a simple idea behind HumaMap: understanding the person alone is not enough. The situation and the people around them matter too.

Person environment fit and Lewin ↗Kristof Brown, Zimmerman & Johnson, 2005 ↗

These sources inform areas HumaMap organises. They do not endorse HumaMap or validate CAN, WILL and HOW as one scientific model. Reflection frameworks are also not treated as equivalent to evidence based assessment. For example, a systematic review found mixed evidence for the Enneagram's reliability and validity. See the review ↗

The difference

HumaMap is not another personality test.

A test gives you a result. HumaMap gives useful context somewhere to live, connect and stay useful in everyday work.

Individual control

Your HumaMap stays with you.

Your HumaMap is your professional profile. Choose what you make visible and who can see it. Use it with the people and companies you work with, and take it with you when your work changes.
