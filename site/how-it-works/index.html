People make more sense in context.

Kurt Lewin's person and environment tradition, and later person environment fit research, reinforce a simple idea behind HumaMap: understanding the person alone is not enough. The situation and the people around them matter too.

Person environment fit and Lewin ↗Kristof Brown, Zimmerman & Johnson, 2005 ↗

These sources inform areas HumaMap organises. They do not endorse HumaMap or validate CAN, WILL and HOW as one scientific model. Reflection frameworks are not treated as equivalent to evidence based assessment. A systematic review, for example, found mixed evidence for the Enneagram's reliability and validity.
