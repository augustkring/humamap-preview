Privacy

I keep data collection on this site deliberately small. This page explains what is collected, why, and how you can ask for it to be changed or deleted.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Newsletter

If you subscribe to Perspectives, I collect the email address you submit, the time and source of the subscription, and the current consent version. I use that information to send the Perspectives emails you asked to receive.

Newsletter emails are sent on the basis of your consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time. Use the unsubscribe link in an email, visit the unsubscribe page, or write to ak@augustkring.com.

Anti-abuse data

The signup endpoint uses a short-lived pseudonymous rate-limit key to reduce automated abuse. The raw IP address is not stored in the newsletter database. Rate-limit records are kept for no more than 24 hours.

Retention

An active subscriber email is kept while the subscription is active. If you unsubscribe, the record is marked as unsubscribed and scheduled for deletion after 30 days. The short delay helps prevent accidental re-mailing while the withdrawal is being propagated.

Service providers

The site and server functions are hosted on Vercel. Newsletter subscription data is stored using Neon. These providers process data only as part of operating the site and newsletter infrastructure. I do not sell subscriber information.

Contact

If you contact me directly, I receive the information you choose to send. I use it to read and respond to your message and keep it only for as long as it remains useful for that conversation or any legitimate follow-up.

Cookies and tracking

augustkring.com does not currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If that changes, this page and the consent experience will be updated before optional tracking is enabled.

Your choices

You can ask what personal information I hold about you, request a correction or deletion, or withdraw newsletter consent by writing to ak@augustkring.com.
