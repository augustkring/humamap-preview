Where I add value

I do my best work in the early stages, when a challenge calls for fresh thinking and clear direction. I combine creative thinking with commercial understanding and strategic judgment to spot opportunities and find better ways forward.

I enjoy giving ambitious ideas direction and bringing people together around a shared vision. But I also like getting hands-on, experimenting and building things myself, using AI to turn ideas into reality.

What drives me is the desire to build towards a positive future, together with bold people, create things that matter and leave the world a little better than I found it.

Define the Opportunity

Before jumping to solutions, I look for what's really missing: an unmet need, a market shift or a problem framed the wrong way. That gives us something specific to explore instead of another vague idea.

Check the Business Case

Who will pay, what will it cost to deliver, and where does the profit come from? I bring hands-on experience with P&L, customer acquisition and commercial initiatives to answer those questions before we invest more.

Choose a Direction

A promising idea still needs hard choices. I help decide who to reach first, how to get to market and what needs testing before we commit. That gives us clear reasons to proceed, change course or stop.

Test and Build

I use AI to turn a plan into something people can respond to. That might be a quick pretotype to test demand, a simple workflow or a first working product. Their feedback helps us decide what to build next.

Build Something Worthwhile

Not everything that can make money is worth building. I look for businesses that solve a real need, stand on their own financially and have a positive impact on people and the planet. That's people, planet and profit in practice.

The shift I see

The next decade will test even the strongest market positions.

AI is changing how companies are built and run. Even market leaders may face unexpected competition from companies a fraction of their size. The challenge is turning AI into a core capability that creates greater value for customers and strengthens the business.

Large organisations can combine their scale and customer trust with startup-like agility. Startups can gain capabilities that once required much larger teams. Both can increase productivity, use capital more efficiently and bring the right ideas to market faster.

But greater capacity doesn't automatically make a company more competitive. AI raises the stakes: it amplifies the value of what a company gets right and the cost of what it gets wrong.

Realising AI's potential means rethinking how companies are organised and led. That takes a customer-centric mindset, a culture that innovates and adapts quickly, and the judgement to prioritise and know when to stop.

The real opportunity isn't AI itself. It's the companies we can build around it.
