Hi, I’m August.

I’ve always been drawn to building things and figuring out how they could work better. Today, that curiosity sits where people, business and technology meet.

How I got here.

Started early

I grew up in an entrepreneurial family with roots in e-business and venture building. It made business feel tangible from an early age, and by my early teens I was already building things with friends.

At 18

After a couple of years working part-time at a VR venue in Copenhagen, I got the chance to run it. I left school and took the job. In a small startup team, I ended up close to almost every part of the business and gradually moved deeper into business development, new commercial initiatives and spinouts.

Learning my limits

I kept taking on more and pushing harder until I burnt out. Recovery took time, but it changed how I think about work, people and myself. It pushed me towards a more human-centred way of building and leading.

Back to building

I eventually returned to operations and rebuilt my capacity. When generative AI began to take off, I started studying what it could mean for how companies are built and run. That led me to KRING and work on AI-native products and agentic AI. Today, I’m building ventures again with people, planet and profit at the core.

Mindset

Be ambitious about the direction and disciplined about the first step.

Move, learn and adjust instead of waiting for certainty.

Learn, unlearn, relearn.

People, planet & profit.

Different lenses. Same person.

None of these define me. I use them to notice patterns in how I think and work, and I pay most attention to where they overlap.

Curious, energetic, structured and direct.

I like exploring new ideas, questioning assumptions and saying what I think. I get energy from people and possibilities, but I also like structure when it helps turn something new into something real.

At work, that usually means I’m comfortable in ambiguity, generate options quickly and enjoy taking ownership. The watch-out is moving too fast or pushing too hard.

Express Builder

I recognise myself in the idea of responding to something real, then building quickly once it feels right. Bigger commitments tend to be better when I let the first reaction settle.

At work, I’m strongest when I can turn an opportunity into visible progress. Conversation, movement and collaboration also help me think.

ENFP

I recognise the pull towards people, possibilities and meaning. I connect ideas quickly, like exploring alternatives and value freedom to find my own way forward.

At work, that points me towards early-stage problems, product shaping and business development, especially when there is room for initiative and a larger purpose.

7w8

I recognise the mix of optimism, intensity and a strong appetite for new possibilities. I can be direct, action-oriented and quick to move towards whatever feels alive.

The useful warning is to stay with difficult things long enough, rather than always moving towards the next interesting possibility.

Manifesting Generator

I recognise the combination of speed and responsiveness. Once something feels worth engaging with, I tend to move quickly and learn through doing.

The useful reminder is that fast execution and fast decisions are not the same thing. Bigger commitments usually benefit from time and clarity.
