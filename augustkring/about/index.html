I’m a Danish Impact / Tech Entrepreneur & Waymaker, building towards a positive future together with bold people.

What I bring

I connect technology, business, product and people to turn emerging opportunities into something worth building.

Agentic AI & Strategy

Find where AI can change the business, then turn it into agents and workflows people can actually use.

Strategic Innovation

Connect shifts in technology, markets and customers to concrete opportunities worth pursuing.

Business Development

Shape the business model, partnerships and commercial path around an opportunity, not just the product.

Product Development

Turn an idea into the smallest viable product that creates real value and teaches us what to build next.

Market Validation & GTM

Test the riskiest assumptions with real behaviour, then turn the evidence into a practical path to market.

Strategic Leadership

Set a clear direction, make the important decisions explicit and give people ownership of the path.

Principles I work by

These are the principles I return to when I build, decide and lead.

Think big. Start small. Scale fast.

Set an ambitious direction. Start with the smallest useful test. Scale what works.

Execution over fear.

Do not wait for certainty. Take the next useful step, learn from what happens and adjust.

Learn, unlearn, relearn.

Keep learning. Let go of what no longer fits. Relearn as the world changes.

People, planet & profit.

Build businesses where economic value, human value and wider impact reinforce each other.

Human-centric by design.

Technology should expand human capability and agency, not make people an afterthought.

What I want to build

Businesses and ideas that make something meaningfully better and can stand on their own economics.

I’m not attached to one industry or format. I’m drawn to problems where technology can create a real step change, especially when a better business can also be better for the people it serves and the world around it.

AI is especially interesting because it changes how much a small team can understand, build and operate. I want to use that leverage to create companies that are more capable, more human and more resilient, not simply more automated.

For the curious

The story so far.

Business and technology were part of the background growing up, including through my family, and I became interested early in how new ideas change companies. I learn best by getting close to the work, forming my own view and testing it in practice.

At 18, I left upper secondary school to run a VR arcade in Copenhagen and became responsible for both the business and a team of around ten people. Customers, staffing, operations, decisions, mistakes and consequences all became real at once. That is not something you learn at business school.

Since then, I have moved across operations, business development, product development and venture building. The common thread is early-stage work: seeing a possibility, figuring out whether it matters, turning it into a first viable version and testing it against reality.

AI has changed how fast I can do that. It lets a small team research, build and learn at a level that used to require far more people and capital. That is why much of my work now sits where AI, business and new company-building meet.

I’m at my best early in the journey, when the answer is not obvious yet: connecting ideas, setting direction, building the first version and learning cheaply and quickly in the market.
