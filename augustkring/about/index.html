Hi, I’m August.

I’m a Danish Impact / Tech Entrepreneur & Waymaker, building towards a positive future together with bold people.

What I bring

I connect technology, business, product and people to turn emerging opportunities into something worth building.

Agentic AI & Strategy

Finding where AI and agents can create the most value, then turning that into practical capability.

Strategic Innovation

Connecting shifts in technology, markets and customer needs to opportunities worth pursuing.

Business Development

Turning opportunities into business models, partnerships and commercial paths that can create and capture value.

Product Development

Turning customer needs into a focused first product, then learning from use.

Market Validation & GTM

Using pretotyping to test demand before committing heavily, then turning the evidence into a path to market.

Strategic Leadership

Creating a direction people want to move towards, then giving them ownership of how they get there.

Principles I work by

These are the principles I return to when I build, decide and lead.

Think big. Start small. Scale fast.

Set an ambitious direction. Start with the smallest useful test. Scale what works.

Execution over fear.

Do not wait for certainty. Take the next useful step, learn from what happens and adjust.

Learn, unlearn, relearn.

Keep learning. Let go of what no longer fits. Relearn as the world changes.

People, planet & profit.

Build businesses where economic value, human value and wider impact reinforce each other.

Human-centric by design.

Technology should expand human capability and agency, not make people an afterthought.

What I’m building toward

I want to spend my time building towards a positive future together with bold people.

I’m interested in ideas, projects, companies and movements that can make something meaningfully better. The form matters less to me than the direction and the value it can create.

Technology, and AI in particular, expands what is possible. I care about it when that new capability helps people build better businesses, solve meaningful problems or create progress that would otherwise have been harder to achieve.

For the curious

The story so far.

When I was 18, I got the opportunity to run a VR arcade in Copenhagen. I left upper secondary school to take it on and became responsible for both the business and a team of around 10 people. It was an early lesson in operations, leadership and what happens when decisions have real consequences.

Over time, my work moved through operations and business development into product and venture building. More recently, much of it has focused on AI strategy, Agentic AI and what new technology changes about how companies can be built and run.

AI has made it possible to move much faster from an idea to something tangible. It has also made one thing even clearer to me: speed only matters if you are learning whether the idea is worth building.

That is still how I like to work: see the possibility, understand why it matters, make it concrete, test it against reality and build from what you learn.
