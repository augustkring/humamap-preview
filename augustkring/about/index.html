Hi, I’m August.

I’m a Danish entrepreneur and Waymaker, driven by curiosity about how people, technology and business can create positive change.

The story so far.

I started early, learnt by doing, pushed too hard, rebuilt — and came back to building with a different perspective.

Early years

I grew up in an entrepreneurial family with roots in e-business and venture building. It sparked an early passion for business and positive change, and by my early teens I was already building things with friends.

At 18

After a couple of years working part-time at a VR venue in Copenhagen, I got the chance to run it. I left school and took the job. In a lean startup environment, I ended up close to almost every part of the business and gradually spent more time on business development, new commercial initiatives and co-founding spinouts.

A reset

I kept taking on more and pushing harder until I burnt out. Recovery took time, but it became one of the most important lessons of my life. It gave me space to understand myself more deeply, personally and spiritually, and shaped a more human-centred view of how I wanted to work and live.

Building again

I eventually returned to operations and rebuilt my capacity. When generative AI began to take off, I became fascinated by what it could mean for how businesses are built and run. That led me to KRING, where I worked on AI-native products and agentic AI alongside people with deep experience in the field. Today, I’m building ventures again, with people, planet and profit at the core.

Skill Set

A multidisciplinary mix of strategy, business and product skills I use to turn new possibilities into something practical.

Agentic AI & Strategy

Spot where AI can materially change the business, then translate that into practical agents, workflows and ways of working.

Strategic Innovation

Connect shifts in technology, markets and customer behaviour to opportunities worth pursuing.

Business Development

Shape the business model, partnerships and commercial path around an opportunity — not just the product.

Product Development

Turn an idea into the smallest useful product that creates value and teaches us what to build next.

Market Validation & GTM

Test the riskiest assumptions with real behaviour, then turn the evidence into a practical path to market.

Strategic Leadership

Create direction, make the important decisions explicit and give people ownership of the path.

Mindset

The principles I return to when I build, decide and lead.

Think big. Start small. Scale fast.

Set an ambitious direction. Start with the smallest useful test. Move fast when the evidence earns it.

Execution over fear.

Uncertainty is a reason to learn, not wait. Take the next useful step, see what reality says and adjust.

Learn, unlearn, relearn.

Stay curious enough to change your mind when the world, the evidence or your own experience changes.

People, planet & profit.

Build so human value, wider impact and sound economics reinforce rather than compete with one another.

Human-centric by design.

Use technology to expand human capability and agency — not reduce people to an afterthought.
