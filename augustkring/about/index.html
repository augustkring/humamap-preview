Hi, I’m August.

I look at how people, technology and businesses develop together, and where connecting them differently can create new opportunities for commercial value and positive impact.

How I got here

From running a business at 18 to developing new ventures today.

I was interested in people, business and technology long before I knew what I wanted to do with them. Even in primary school, I kept coming back to how people develop, why businesses work and what new technology makes possible.

At 18, I left upper secondary school to run a VR arcade in Copenhagen. I was responsible for daily operations, customers and a team of around ten people. It gave me a first-hand education in leadership, commercial decisions and the demands of keeping a business running.

That experience took me towards business development, product development and venture building. I later worked with AI in KRING's venture environment, where I saw what smaller teams could explore and build when technology became part of how they worked.

Today, I work across several connected initiatives: Blentera, a platform being developed to coordinate AI agents; August Works, which helps established businesses put AI to work; HumaMap, which helps people and teams understand how they work; and Waymaking, where I explore ventures with commercial potential and positive impact.

The projects are different, but the question behind them is similar: how do we create something useful for people that can also stand on its own commercially? That is where my interest in human development, business strategy and building new things comes together.

What I bring

Principles I work by

Simple rules for deciding what matters and making progress without unnecessary complexity.

See the whole picture.

Consider the people, technology and economics together before settling on a solution.

Start with real needs.

Begin with a problem people care about, not a technology looking for a use.

Make ideas tangible.

Build the first useful version, put it in front of people and learn from what they do.

Stay open to change.

Challenge your assumptions and change course when evidence points somewhere better.

Make impact sustainable.

Look for business models where value for customers and positive impact can reinforce each other.

What I want to build

Businesses where commercial success and positive impact can reinforce each other.

I don't start with a particular industry or technology. I start with a problem worth solving and look at how people's needs, business models and new capabilities could come together in a better way.

That might become a product, a different way of working or a new company. The ambition is the same: something people genuinely value, with economics strong enough to sustain it.

AI gives small teams more capacity to research, build and operate. I want to use that advantage to explore worthwhile ideas, test them earlier and make better decisions about what deserves to grow.
