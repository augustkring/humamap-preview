People are different. That should be a strength at work.

HumaMap helps make those differences easier to understand and use, so people can show more of what they bring and companies can make better decisions about how people work together.

More than what you can do.

A CV can show your experience, skills and qualifications. It says much less about how you work, what drives you, how you take ownership and how you work with others.

HumaMap helps you put that part of your professional identity into words and take it with you from one role or company to the next.

Technology should help us understand people better.

AI makes it possible to keep useful context about how people work available when it is needed. A manager cannot remember every person's working style, motivation and relationships all the time.

We use AI to help people understand themselves, help colleagues understand each other and help leaders work differently with different people.
