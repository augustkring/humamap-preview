People are different. That should be a strength at work.

HumaMap helps people understand how they work and helps companies understand the people behind the roles. So people can show more of what they bring, and companies can build stronger teams.

More than what you can do.

A CV can show your experience, skills and qualifications. It says much less about how you think, what drives you, how you take ownership or how you work with other people.

HumaMap helps you put words to that part of your professional identity, and take it with you as your career changes.

Technology should help us understand people better.

AI can make useful people context available when it matters. A manager cannot keep every person's working style, motivation and relationships in their head all the time.

HumaMap uses AI to help people understand themselves, colleagues understand each other and leaders work better with different people.
