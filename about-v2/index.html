People are different. That should be a strength at work.

HumaMap exists to make the human side of work easier to understand and use, so people can bring more of what makes them valuable and companies can build stronger teams around the people they have.

More than what you can do.

A CV can show your experience, skills and qualifications. It says much less about how you think, what drives you, how you take ownership or how you work with other people.

HumaMap gives people a way to understand and express that part of their professional identity, and to take it with them throughout their working life.

Technology should help us understand people better.

AI makes it possible to work with human context at a scale that was difficult before. A manager cannot keep every person's working style, motivation, relationships and context in their head all the time.

We want to use that capability to help people understand themselves, help colleagues understand each other and help leaders adapt how they work with different people.

01

Understand yourself

Give people language for how they work and what they bring.

02

Understand each other

Make differences easier to see before they become avoidable friction.

03

Lead with context

Help leaders adapt to different people instead of using the same approach for everyone.

Not to let algorithms manage people. To give people better context for human decisions. HumaMap gives context. People make the decisions.

Better for people. Stronger for companies.

Human-centricity is not a soft alternative to performance. When people are better understood, work can become healthier and companies can become more robust.

  • Take greater ownership of how you work
For companies

More context for better people decisions.

  • Understand the people behind the roles

HumaMap works most directly around the first two: using technology to strengthen people and the companies they work in.
